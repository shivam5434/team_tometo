    i

    Estimated air quality for any place in India on the CPCB AQI scale. These are estimates from a regional air-quality model (about 45 km resolution), not readings from a sensor near you, and can differ from official CPCB or station-network numbers.

    AQI = the highest CPCB sub-index among PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, O₃ and CO. Markers use the latest model values; CPCB itself uses 24-hour averages (8-hour for O₃ and CO), shown separately on each place. General information, not medical advice.

    Heatmap mode: your browser asks Open-Meteo directly for about 360 grid points (1° apart, over land near our cities) and blends them into a smooth colour field. Nothing is stored on our server. The colours between grid points are interpolated guesses, and are cached in your browser for an hour. Open-Meteo’s free tier is for non-commercial use.
